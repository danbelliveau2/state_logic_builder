# Intermediate Representation — Magnet_Feed
Station number: 1
Description: Feeds magnets from a dial stack: hold-down clamps the stack, vertical shuttle lifts it, horizontal shuttle presents the top magnet (coin-changer style). Retries presentation up to 3x on missing magnet, then declares the stack exhausted and requests a dial index. Top retainer holds the magnet down during every horizontal shuttle move (extend before horizontal extend, retract after shuttle-over) so it cannot magnetize and flip up.
